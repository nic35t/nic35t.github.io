{%- comment -%} Param: post. A tile's one-line summary. Jekyll's auto-excerpt is the post's first block, and 29 posts open with a "## 시작하며:" heading, so their excerpt would be a heading, not a sentence. When the excerpt holds a heading, take the first paragraph after the first h2 instead (empty if there is none, and the tile then shows no excerpt). A blank `excerpt: ""`, which the CMS and `npm run new` can leave behind, takes the same fallback instead of hiding the summary.
