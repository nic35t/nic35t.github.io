It used to be a plain stylesheet, which meant nothing could be drawn until a third-party CDN had completed a round trip — for every visitor, on every page. Now the page paints in the fallback and Pretendard swaps in when it arrives. The trade is one visible font swap, and the fallback is what makes it acceptable: the stack goes -apple-system, Malgun Gothic, Roboto — the Korean UI font of whichever platform the reader is on, not a generic serif. Every screenshot taken while building this site rendered through that fallback, because the CDN is unreachable from the build sandbox, and the typography held up throughout.
