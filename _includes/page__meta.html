{%- comment -%} Date and read-time line for archive rows and the (unused) page hero. Posts themselves use post-byline.html; the two agree on format and arithmetic. - No Font Awesome icons. The calendar and clock glyphs came from a deferred sheet that the redesign removes, and the text already says what it is. - Locale decides the date format. It was hardcoded to "%B %-d, %Y", which printed English month names on a Korean site. - On a Korean site read time counts characters (about 500 a minute) and prints "N분 소요" / "1분 미만" (spec 9.1). The theme's words-per-minute figure is an English one and printed "1 분 소요" with a stray space; it stays only for other locales. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign document = post | default: page -%} {%- if document.read_time or document.show_date -%} {%- if site.locale contains "ko" -%} {%- assign date_format = "%Y년 %-m월 %-d일" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign date_format = "%B %-d, %Y" -%} {%- endif -%}
